# Camera Angles & Shot Types — Seedance2 Reference

Complete shot taxonomy for Seedance2 prompt engineering. Use these exact keywords in your prompts.

---

## ESTABLISHING & WIDE SHOTS

### Extreme Wide Shot (EWS)
- **Prompt keyword:** `extreme wide shot`, `EWS`, `establishing shot`
- **Effect:** Subject dwarfed by environment; sets scale and location
- **Best for:** Opening scenes, landscapes, epic sequences, sci-fi, westerns
- **Example:** `extreme wide shot of a lone figure walking through a vast desert at sunset, cinematic`

### Wide Shot (WS) / Full Shot
- **Prompt keyword:** `wide shot`, `full shot`, `long shot`
- **Effect:** Full body visible with environment context; shows geography
- **Best for:** Action scenes, dance, sports, revealing location
- **Example:** `wide shot of a dancer performing in an empty warehouse, dramatic lighting`

### Master Shot
- **Prompt keyword:** `master shot`, `coverage shot`
- **Effect:** Captures entire scene geography; used as editorial anchor
- **Best for:** Dialogue scenes, group interactions, spatial orientation
- **Example:** `master shot of three people at a dining table, warm restaurant lighting`

---

## MEDIUM SHOTS

### Medium Wide Shot (MWS)
- **Prompt keyword:** `medium wide shot`, `cowboy shot`, `knee shot`
- **Effect:** Waist-to-knees frame; balance of person and environment
- **Best for:** Westerns, action heroes, character-environment relationship
- **Example:** `cowboy shot of a detective walking down a rain-soaked alley, noir style`

### Medium Shot (MS)
- **Prompt keyword:** `medium shot`, `waist shot`, `mid shot`
- **Effect:** Waist up; standard conversational framing
- **Best for:** Dialogue, interviews, presentations, social interaction
- **Example:** `medium shot of a scientist explaining research, laboratory background, natural light`

### Medium Close-Up (MCU)
- **Prompt keyword:** `medium close-up`, `MCU`, `chest shot`
- **Effect:** Chest to head; more intimate than medium, less intense than close-up
- **Best for:** TV drama, newscasts, character reaction shots
- **Example:** `medium close-up of an actor delivering emotional monologue, soft bokeh background`

---

## CLOSE SHOTS

### Close-Up (CU)
- **Prompt keyword:** `close-up`, `CU`, `face shot`
- **Effect:** Face fills frame; reveals emotion and micro-expressions
- **Best for:** Emotional moments, tension, character studies, dialogue
- **Example:** `close-up of a woman's face as she reads a letter, tears forming, shallow depth of field`

### Extreme Close-Up (ECU)
- **Prompt keyword:** `extreme close-up`, `ECU`, `macro shot`, `detail shot`
- **Effect:** Single feature or object fills frame; hyper-detail
- **Best for:** Tension, horror, suspense, detail emphasis, product reveal
- **Example:** `extreme close-up of an eye reflecting flames, hyper-detailed, 8K`

### Insert Shot
- **Prompt keyword:** `insert shot`, `cutaway`, `detail insert`
- **Effect:** Isolated detail with narrative significance
- **Best for:** Revealing clues, emphasizing objects, montage sequences
- **Example:** `insert shot of hands exchanging a worn leather wallet, noir lighting`

---

## ANGLE-BASED SHOTS

### Bird's Eye View / Top-Down
- **Prompt keyword:** `bird's eye view`, `top-down shot`, `overhead shot`, `God's eye view`
- **Effect:** Looking straight down; reduces subjects to patterns and shapes
- **Best for:** Choreography, crowds, architecture, surreal sequences, maps
- **Example:** `bird's eye view of a busy intersection at night, city lights, time-lapse motion blur`

### High Angle Shot
- **Prompt keyword:** `high angle shot`, `looking down`, `elevated angle`
- **Effect:** Camera above subject looking down; makes subject feel small, vulnerable
- **Best for:** Portraying weakness, surveillance, child perspective from adult POV
- **Example:** `high angle shot looking down at a child alone in a schoolyard, melancholic mood`

### Eye Level Shot
- **Prompt keyword:** `eye level shot`, `eye level camera`, `natural angle`
- **Effect:** Neutral, relatable perspective; audience equals subject
- **Best for:** Everyday scenes, documentary feel, dialogue, realism
- **Example:** `eye level shot of two friends talking at a café, golden hour window light`

### Low Angle Shot
- **Prompt keyword:** `low angle shot`, `looking up`, `upward angle`
- **Effect:** Camera below subject looking up; makes subject feel powerful, imposing
- **Best for:** Heroes, villains, authority figures, architecture, intimidation
- **Example:** `low angle shot looking up at a skyscraper in rain, dramatic storm clouds, cinematic`

### Worm's Eye View
- **Prompt keyword:** `worm's eye view`, `ground level shot`, `extreme low angle`
- **Effect:** Ground-level perspective; maximum subject dominance
- **Best for:** Giants, superheroes, empowerment, architectural scale
- **Example:** `worm's eye view of a crowd marching forward, sky background, powerful composition`

### Dutch Angle / Canted Shot
- **Prompt keyword:** `dutch angle`, `canted angle`, `tilted frame`, `oblique angle`
- **Effect:** Camera tilted on its axis; creates unease, disorientation, tension
- **Best for:** Horror, thriller, psychological drama, villain introduction
- **Example:** `dutch angle close-up of a villain smiling, harsh shadows, thriller atmosphere`

## PERSPECTIVE & RELATIONSHIP SHOTS

### Point of View (POV)
- **Prompt keyword:** `POV shot`, `first-person perspective`, `subjective camera`
- **Effect:** Camera IS the character's eyes; maximum immersion
- **Best for:** VR-style content, horror, action, immersive storytelling
- **Example:** `POV shot walking through a dark forest, flashlight beam, twigs snapping underfoot`

### Over-the-Shoulder (OTS)
- **Prompt keyword:** `over-the-shoulder shot`, `OTS`, `shoulder shot`
- **Effect:** Camera behind one character facing another; conversational anchor
- **Best for:** Dialogue scenes, interviews, confrontations, relationships
- **Example:** `over-the-shoulder shot during tense negotiation, blurred foreground shoulder, sharp focus on opposing face`

### Two Shot
- **Prompt keyword:** `two shot`, `double shot`
- **Effect:** Two subjects in same frame; shows relationship and dynamic
- **Best for:** Couples, partnerships, conflicts, comparisons
- **Example:** `two shot of a detective and suspect across an interrogation table, harsh overhead light`

### Group Shot / Ensemble Shot
- **Prompt keyword:** `group shot`, `ensemble shot`, `crowd shot`
- **Effect:** Multiple subjects unified in frame
- **Best for:** Team introductions, family scenes, social gatherings
- **Example:** `group shot of a street band performing, warm bokeh city lights, dynamic energy`

### Reaction Shot
- **Prompt keyword:** `reaction shot`, `cutaway reaction`
- **Effect:** Shows character's emotional response to off-screen action
- **Best for:** Comedy, drama, storytelling beats, reveals
- **Example:** `reaction shot of a crowd watching a magic trick, expressions of amazement`

## SPECIALTY SHOTS

### Aerial Shot
- **Prompt keyword:** `aerial shot`, `drone shot`, `aerial view`
- **Effect:** From aircraft/drone height; grand scale, geography
- **Best for:** Openings, transitions, epic scale, travel content
- **Example:** `aerial drone shot sweeping over a coastal city at dawn, golden light reflecting on water`

### Shoulder-Mounted / Handheld
- **Prompt keyword:** `handheld camera`, `shoulder-mounted`, `documentary style`, `verite`
- **Effect:** Organic camera shake; raw, authentic, immersive feel
- **Best for:** War films, documentaries, chase scenes, indie drama
- **Example:** `handheld close-up following a journalist through a crowded protest, chaotic energy`

### Profile Shot
- **Prompt keyword:** `profile shot`, `side angle`, `90-degree angle`
- **Effect:** Subject facing 90° from camera; classic portraiture feel
- **Best for:** Portraiture, silhouettes, contemplative moments
- **Example:** `profile shot of a musician playing violin, window backlight, silhouette detail`

### Silhouette Shot
- **Prompt keyword:** `silhouette shot`, `backlit silhouette`, `contre-jour`
- **Effect:** Subject dark against bright background; shape over detail
- **Best for:** Mood, mystery, romance, epic moments, sunsets
- **Example:** `silhouette of two figures embracing against a blazing sunset horizon, cinematic`

### Candid / Hidden Camera Style
- **Prompt keyword:** `candid shot`, `voyeuristic angle`, `hidden camera style`
- **Effect:** Unaware subject; documentary realism, authenticity
- **Best for:** Slice-of-life, documentary, naturalistic drama
- **Example:** `candid-style medium shot of a street musician, telephoto compression, natural light`

### Split Diopter Shot
- **Prompt keyword:** `split diopter`, `split focus`, `dual focus shot`
- **Effect:** Two planes of action both in sharp focus simultaneously
- **Best for:** Parallel action, suspense, thriller reveals
- **Example:** `split diopter shot keeping both foreground phone and background figure in sharp focus`
