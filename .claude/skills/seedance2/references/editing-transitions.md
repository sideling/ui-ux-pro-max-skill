# Cuts, Transitions & Editing Techniques — Seedance2 Reference

Professional editing vocabulary for Seedance2 sequences and multi-shot storytelling.

---

## FUNDAMENTAL CUTS

### Straight Cut (Hard Cut)
- **Prompt keyword:** `cut to`, `hard cut`, `straight cut`
- **Effect:** Instantaneous switch between two shots; most common edit
- **Best for:** Continuous action, maintaining pace, standard narrative flow
- **Rhythm:** Neutral — doesn't add emotion, lets content speak
- **Example sequence:** `...wide shot of city → cut to close-up of a door opening...`

### Jump Cut
- **Prompt keyword:** `jump cut`, `discontinuous cut`, `jarring cut`
- **Effect:** Cut between similar shots with slight time/position change; disorienting, unstable
- **Best for:** Anxiety, fragmented memory, time passing, social media aesthetic
- **Notable use:** Godard's "Breathless", YouTube vlogs, panic sequences
- **Example:** `jump cut series showing a character's sleepless night, disjointed time`

### Match Cut
- **Prompt keyword:** `match cut`, `visual match cut`, `form cut`, `shape cut`
- **Effect:** Two shots share similar shape/action/composition creating seamless link
- **Best for:** Poetic transitions, thematic connections, elegant scene changes
- **Classic example:** Bone → spaceship (2001: A Space Odyssey)
- **Example:** `match cut from spinning coin to spinning planet, seamless cosmic transition`

### Action Cut (Cut on Action)
- **Prompt keyword:** `cut on action`, `action cut`, `motion cut`
- **Effect:** Cut mid-movement creating seamless continuity; viewer's eye stays on motion
- **Best for:** Door openings, punches, standing up, any physical action
- **Rule:** Start the action in Shot A, complete it in Shot B
- **Example:** `character begins to sit down → cut to them fully seated from new angle`

## AUDIO-DRIVEN CUTS

### J-Cut (Audio Lead)
- **Prompt keyword:** `J-cut`, `audio lead`, `sound bridge in`
- **Effect:** Audio of next scene starts before picture cuts; smooth, sophisticated
- **Best for:** Dialogue transitions, documentary, news, professional narrative
- **Why J-shaped:** On audio timeline, audio precedes video like the letter J
- **Example:** `café ambience begins while still showing apartment exterior → cut to café interior`

### L-Cut (Audio Tail)
- **Prompt keyword:** `L-cut`, `audio tail`, `sound bridge out`
- **Effect:** Audio of current scene continues after picture cuts to new scene
- **Best for:** Emotional resonance, conversation reactions, lingering atmosphere
- **Why L-shaped:** On timeline, audio extends past video like the letter L
- **Example:** `character's speech continues as camera cuts to listener's reaction`

## IMPACT CUTS

### Smash Cut
- **Prompt keyword:** `smash cut`, `shock cut`, `impact cut`
- **Effect:** Sudden, jarring cut to contrasting scene; maximum surprise
- **Best for:** Comedy punchlines, horror reveals, dream-to-reality, shocking contrast
- **Example:** `peaceful sleeping face → smash cut to alarm blaring close-up`

### Graphic Match Cut
- **Prompt keyword:** `graphic match`, `visual rhyme cut`, `color match cut`
- **Effect:** Two shots share identical visual element (color, shape, composition)
- **Best for:** Thematic poetry, music videos, artistic transitions
- **Example:** `red sun setting → graphic match cut to red apple being bitten`

## RHYTHMIC & MONTAGE CUTS

### Cross-Cut (Parallel Editing)
- **Prompt keyword:** `cross-cut`, `parallel cut`, `intercutting`, `parallel action`
- **Effect:** Alternates between two simultaneous storylines; builds tension
- **Best for:** Last-minute rescues, phone conversations, parallel storylines
- **Classic use:** Chase scene intercut with victim trying to escape
- **Example:** `cross-cutting between a surgeon operating and family waiting anxiously`

### Montage
- **Prompt keyword:** `montage sequence`, `training montage`, `time-lapse montage`, `rapid montage`
- **Effect:** Series of short clips showing compressed time or thematic accumulation
- **Types:**
  - **Soviet montage** — ideological meaning from juxtaposition
  - **Continuity montage** — time compression (training, travel)
  - **Thematic montage** — building a concept through images
- **Example:** `training montage showing athlete's progression from novice to champion, upbeat energy`

### Cutting to the Beat
- **Prompt keyword:** `cut to beat`, `rhythm cut`, `music-driven cut`, `sync cut`
- **Effect:** Edits sync with music beats; energizing, satisfying
- **Best for:** Music videos, trailers, highlight reels, commercials
- **Example:** `rapid cuts synced to drum beats showing city energy at night`

## OPTICAL TRANSITIONS

### Dissolve (Cross-Dissolve)
- **Prompt keyword:** `dissolve`, `cross-dissolve`, `fade through`, `soft transition`
- **Effect:** Shot A fades out while Shot B fades in simultaneously; gentle, lyrical
- **Best for:** Time passing, memory, dreams, emotional warmth, nostalgia
- **Example:** `dissolve from summer meadow to snow-covered same location, seasons change`

### Fade to Black
- **Prompt keyword:** `fade to black`, `fade out`, `fade in from black`
- **Effect:** Image darkens to black (or emerges from); signals chapter end/beginning
- **Best for:** Act breaks, serious endings, ellipsis in time, gravitas
- **Example:** `slow fade to black after final dialogue, then fade in on morning scene`

### Fade to White
- **Prompt keyword:** `fade to white`, `whiteout`, `overexposed fade`
- **Effect:** Image brightens to white; dreamlike, heavenly, blinding
- **Best for:** Dreams, near-death, flashbacks, ethereal tone
- **Example:** `fade to white as character loses consciousness, memory sequence begins`

### Wipe
- **Prompt keyword:** `wipe transition`, `linear wipe`, `page turn wipe`
- **Effect:** New scene pushes old one off screen via a line
- **Best for:** Action films, retro aesthetic (Star Wars), comic book feel
- **Variations:**
  - `horizontal wipe` — left to right or right to left
  - `vertical wipe` — top or bottom
  - `diagonal wipe` — energetic
  - `iris wipe` — circle expands or contracts
- **Example:** `star wars-style wipe transition between battle and command center scenes`

### Iris In / Iris Out
- **Prompt keyword:** `iris in`, `iris out`, `circular wipe`, `vignette transition`
- **Effect:** Circular mask grows (in) or shrinks (out) to/from black
- **Best for:** Silent film homage, children's content, vintage feel, focusing attention
- **Example:** `iris out to black closing on character's face, silent film style`

### Morph Transition
- **Prompt keyword:** `morph transition`, `shape morph`, `seamless morph`
- **Effect:** Subject A smoothly transforms into subject B
- **Best for:** Transformation sequences, music videos, VFX, surrealism
- **Example:** `morph transition from caterpillar to butterfly, smooth metamorphosis`

### Whip Pan Transition
- **Prompt keyword:** `whip pan transition`, `swish pan cut`, `motion blur transition`
- **Effect:** Fast pan creates motion blur used as transition between scenes
- **Best for:** Energetic narrative links, comedy, action, scene changes
- **Example:** `whip pan right blurs to white → new scene begins from matching whip pan`

## MATCH-BASED TRANSITIONS

### Match on Action
- **Prompt keyword:** `match on action`, `eyeline match`, `matched edit`
- **Effect:** Continuous action bridges two shots/scenes seamlessly
- **Best for:** Spatial continuity, elegant scene transitions
- **Example:** `character reaches for door in Scene A → hand grasps different handle in Scene B`

### Thematic Cut
- **Prompt keyword:** `thematic cut`, `concept cut`, `idea cut`
- **Effect:** Two shots connected by shared theme, idea, or symbol
- **Best for:** Poetic filmmaking, art-house, documentary essays
- **Example:** `child blowing out birthday candles → soldier lighting a flare, both about fire and hope`

---

## TIME-BASED TECHNIQUES

### Flashback Cut
- **Prompt keyword:** `flashback cut`, `memory cut`, `flashback transition`
- **Effect:** Transition to earlier time period, often with visual treatment
- **Visual treatments:** Soft focus, desaturation, grain, color shift, slow-motion
- **Example:** `character touches a photograph → flashback cut to sepia-toned memory scene`

### Flash Forward
- **Prompt keyword:** `flash forward`, `premonition cut`, `future flash`
- **Effect:** Brief jump to future event; used for foreshadowing
- **Best for:** Thrillers, non-linear storytelling, mystery
- **Example:** `flash forward of explosion → back to present as character enters building`

### Freeze Frame Cut
- **Prompt keyword:** `freeze frame`, `still frame transition`, `frozen moment`
- **Effect:** Motion stops on a single frame; used for emphasis or humor
- **Best for:** Comedy introductions, dramatic beats, title cards
- **Example:** `freeze frame on character mid-jump as title card appears`

### Slow-Motion Cut
- **Prompt keyword:** `slow motion`, `slow-mo transition`, `overcranked`
- **Effect:** Time stretched; heightens emotion, beauty, or impact
- **Best for:** Action climax, emotional peaks, beauty shots, violence stylization
- **Example:** `slow-motion cut to glass shattering as argument reaches breaking point`

## AVANT-GARDE & SPECIALTY

### Subliminal Cut
- **Prompt keyword:** `subliminal flash`, `subliminal cut`, `single-frame insert`
- **Effect:** Insert of 1–3 frames of contrasting image; subliminal impact
- **Best for:** Horror (brief glimpse of monster), psychological thrillers
- **Example:** `subliminal flash of a skull during unsettling conversation, barely perceptible`

### Invisible Cut (Hidden Cut)
- **Prompt keyword:** `invisible cut`, `hidden cut`, `oner cut`, `single take style`
- **Effect:** Cut disguised by pan, darkness, or object pass to appear as one continuous shot
- **Best for:** Immersive storytelling, virtuosic filmmaking, tension
- **Example:** `invisible cut hidden in a rapid pan through a doorframe, seamless transition`

### Color Grade Cut
- **Prompt keyword:** `color grade shift`, `tonal shift transition`, `palette transition`
- **Effect:** Transition marked by dramatic color temperature or grade change
- **Best for:** Timeline jumps, perspective shifts, tone changes
- **Example:** `warm golden grade shifts to cold blue tones as character enters villain's world`
