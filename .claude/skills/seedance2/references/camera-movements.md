# Camera Movements — Seedance2 Reference

Complete movement vocabulary for Seedance2 prompts. Combine movements with shot types for precise cinematic control.

---

## HORIZONTAL MOVEMENTS

### Pan (Horizontal Pivot)
- **Prompt keyword:** `camera pans left`, `camera pans right`, `slow pan`, `whip pan`
- **Effect:** Camera rotates horizontally on fixed axis; scans or follows action
- **Best for:** Following subjects, landscape reveals, scanning a scene
- **Variations:**
  - `slow pan` — contemplative reveal, landscapes
  - `fast pan` / `swish pan` — energetic transitions, sports
  - `whip pan` — ultra-fast; disorienting; used as cut replacement
- **Example:** `slow pan across a mountain ridge at sunrise, left to right, cinematic golden hour`

### Whip Pan / Swish Pan
- **Prompt keyword:** `whip pan`, `swish pan`, `flash pan`, `snap pan`
- **Effect:** Extremely fast horizontal pan creating motion blur; used as in-camera cut
- **Best for:** Comedy timing, energetic edits, action sequences, scene transitions
- **Example:** `whip pan from one character to another during a comedic reaction, motion blur`

### Truck / Track (Lateral Dolly)
- **Prompt keyword:** `camera trucks left`, `camera trucks right`, `lateral tracking`, `side tracking shot`
- **Effect:** Camera moves laterally (sideways) while keeping same orientation; parallax depth
- **Best for:** Revealing environment, tracking a subject moving sideways, depth showcase
- **Example:** `lateral tracking shot of a runner in slow motion, stadium background blurring`

## FORWARD & BACKWARD MOVEMENTS

### Dolly In (Push In)
- **Prompt keyword:** `dolly in`, `push in`, `camera moves forward`, `slow push in`
- **Effect:** Camera physically moves toward subject; builds intimacy, focus, tension
- **Best for:** Emotional reveals, dramatic moments, horror tension, drawing attention
- **Example:** `slow dolly in on a grieving woman's face, soft focus background, emotional weight`

### Dolly Out (Pull Back)
- **Prompt keyword:** `dolly out`, `pull back`, `camera pulls back`, `reveals reveal`
- **Effect:** Camera moves away from subject; reveals context, creates isolation
- **Best for:** Revealing scale, isolation moments, surprising context reveals
- **Example:** `pull back from a single candle flame to reveal an entire memorial, wide cathedral`

### Dolly Zoom / Vertigo Effect
- **Prompt keyword:** `dolly zoom`, `vertigo effect`, `Hitchcock zoom`, `contra zoom`
- **Effect:** Dolly out while zooming in (or vice versa); background changes scale while subject stays same
- **Best for:** Psychological disorientation, horror, revelation, vertigo moments
- **Example:** `dolly zoom effect on a character's face as they receive shocking news, vertigo distortion`

### Tracking Shot (Follow)
- **Prompt keyword:** `tracking shot`, `follow shot`, `camera follows`, `pursuit shot`
- **Effect:** Camera moves with subject, maintaining framing; maintains energy
- **Best for:** Walking scenes, chase sequences, following action, momentum
- **Example:** `tracking shot following a chef moving through a busy kitchen, continuous movement`

## VERTICAL MOVEMENTS

### Tilt (Vertical Pivot)
- **Prompt keyword:** `camera tilts up`, `camera tilts down`, `slow tilt`, `tilt reveal`
- **Effect:** Camera pivots up or down on fixed axis; reveals vertical scale
- **Best for:** Revealing tall structures, characters standing up, skyward reveals
- **Variations:**
  - `tilt up` — reveals height, aspiration, scale
  - `tilt down` — reveals depth, vulnerability, falling
- **Example:** `camera tilts up slowly from the base of a skyscraper to its peak, dusk sky`

### Pedestal Up / Down (Vertical Dolly)
- **Prompt keyword:** `pedestal up`, `pedestal down`, `camera rises`, `camera descends`
- **Effect:** Camera physically moves up or down while maintaining orientation; smooth elevation change
- **Best for:** Rising above crowds, descending into spaces, vertical reveals
- **Example:** `camera pedestal up from street level rising above the rooftops, city panorama reveal`

### Crane Shot
- **Prompt keyword:** `crane shot`, `jib shot`, `camera cranes up`, `sweeping crane`
- **Effect:** Sweeping vertical and horizontal movement via crane arm; grand, cinematic
- **Best for:** Establishing shots, musical numbers, grand reveals, epic scale
- **Example:** `crane shot rising from two characters embracing up to reveal the entire city skyline`

## CIRCULAR & ARC MOVEMENTS

### Arc Shot (Orbit)
- **Prompt keyword:** `arc shot`, `orbit shot`, `camera orbits`, `360 orbit`, `circular movement`
- **Effect:** Camera circles around a subject; reveals all sides, shows relationships
- **Best for:** Character introductions, dramatic standoffs, product reveals, bullet-time style
- **Example:** `slow arc shot circling around a lone figure standing in a foggy field`

### 360° Spin
- **Prompt keyword:** `360 spin`, `full rotation`, `spinning camera`, `barrel roll camera`
- **Effect:** Complete revolution around subject; disorienting, energetic
- **Best for:** Transformation sequences, action, music videos, VFX moments
- **Example:** `360-degree spin around a fighter in slow motion, debris frozen in air`

## AERIAL & DRONE MOVEMENTS

### Drone Rise
- **Prompt keyword:** `drone rises`, `aerial ascent`, `camera ascends`, `rising drone shot`
- **Effect:** Vertical rise revealing increasing scale of environment
- **Best for:** Establishing location grandeur, openings, epic reveals
- **Example:** `drone shot slowly ascending above a mountain village at dawn, mist in valleys`

### Drone Reveal (Forward Fly)
- **Prompt keyword:** `drone flies forward`, `aerial forward motion`, `droning through`
- **Effect:** Low forward drone movement revealing what's ahead; immersive
- **Best for:** Travel content, flythrough reveals, following roads/rivers
- **Example:** `low drone shot flying forward over a winding forest road at golden hour`

### Drone Descent / Dive
- **Prompt keyword:** `drone descends`, `aerial dive`, `camera dives`, `swoop down`
- **Effect:** Drops from height toward a target; dramatic, immersive
- **Best for:** Reveals, dramatic arrivals, intensifying focus on a subject
- **Example:** `aerial drone dives from above clouds down toward a solo figure on a beach`

## SPECIALTY MOVEMENTS

### Steadicam / Gimbal Walk
- **Prompt keyword:** `steadicam`, `gimbal shot`, `floating camera`, `smooth follow`
- **Effect:** Smooth handheld movement; floats through space with stability
- **Best for:** Long takes, corridor sequences, following characters naturally
- **Example:** `steadicam shot following a character through a bustling nightclub, fluid movement`

### Handheld / Verité
- **Prompt keyword:** `handheld`, `shaky cam`, `documentary handheld`, `vérité style`
- **Effect:** Organic camera shake; raw, immediate, documentary feel
- **Best for:** War sequences, chaos, urgency, documentary aesthetics
- **Example:** `handheld medium shot of medics running through a field, urgent and chaotic`

### Rack Focus (Focus Pull)
- **Prompt keyword:** `rack focus`, `focus pull`, `depth shift`, `shallow depth shift`
- **Effect:** Changes focus plane during shot; directs viewer attention dynamically
- **Best for:** Revealing hidden elements, shifting narrative focus, elegant transitions
- **Example:** `rack focus from a blurry rose in foreground to a sharp face in background`

### Zoom In / Out
- **Prompt keyword:** `zoom in`, `zoom out`, `lens zoom`, `optical zoom`
- **Effect:** Focal length change; different feel from physical dolly movement
- **Best for:** Surveillance feel, 70s retro aesthetic, dramatic emphasis
- **Variations:**
  - `slow zoom in` — creeping dread, intensity
  - `fast zoom in` — comedy, impact
  - `crash zoom` — sudden shock, horror
- **Example:** `slow zoom in on a suspicious figure across the street, telephoto compression`

### Crash Zoom
- **Prompt keyword:** `crash zoom`, `smash zoom`, `rapid zoom`
- **Effect:** Extremely fast zoom to close-up; jarring, comedic, or shocking
- **Best for:** Horror reveals, comedy punctuation, intense sports moments
- **Example:** `crash zoom on a character's face at the moment of realization`

### Time-Lapse Camera Move
- **Prompt keyword:** `time-lapse with camera movement`, `motion time-lapse`, `hyperlapse`
- **Effect:** Time compressed with simultaneous camera movement
- **Best for:** Urban energy, nature transformation, passage of time
- **Example:** `hyperlapse moving through a city street as day transitions to night, light trails`

### Parallax / Ken Burns
- **Prompt keyword:** `parallax motion`, `Ken Burns effect`, `slow drift`, `subtle drift`
- **Effect:** Subtle drift across static or 2D image creates depth illusion
- **Best for:** Photo-to-video, documentary, news, archival footage
- **Example:** `Ken Burns slow pan and zoom on a vintage photograph, warm grain overlay`

### Shoulder Roll
- **Prompt keyword:** `shoulder roll`, `Dutch tilt pan`, `rolling camera`
- **Effect:** Camera rotates on its z-axis while moving; dizzying, psychedelic
- **Best for:** Hallucinatory sequences, music videos, surrealism
- **Example:** `camera rolls while following a spiraling dancer, dreamlike quality`
